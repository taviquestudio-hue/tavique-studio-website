Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Tavique Studio handles information shared by visitors through this website.

1. About Tavique Studio

Tavique Studio is a digital marketing agency based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This website provides information about our services and allows visitors to contact us regarding their hotel or resort marketing requirements.

2. Information you choose to share

If you contact us through our website, email, phone or WhatsApp, you may choose to share information such as:

  • Your name and contact number.
  • Your email address.
  • Your hotel or resort name and location.
  • The services you are interested in.
  • Any additional information included in your enquiry.

We use this information to understand your requirements, respond to enquiries and communicate about potential services.

3. Website enquiry form

When you submit the contact form, your browser prepares a message and opens WhatsApp with the information you entered. You can review the message before sending it.

The website itself does not store the form submission in a database. Once you send the message, WhatsApp's own privacy policy and data practices apply.

4. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website uses Google Tag Manager with container ID GTM-W48LX433. Google Tag Manager allows us to manage website tracking and measurement tools.

The information collected depends on which tags and analytics services are configured and activated in the container. These services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Before enabling analytics or advertising tags, we should review the applicable consent requirements and configure appropriate privacy controls.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate communication, analytics or website services, subject to the applicable terms and requirements.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, or as required by applicable law.
