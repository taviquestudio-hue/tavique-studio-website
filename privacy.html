Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Tavique Studio may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: November 2026

01 / INFORMATION HOW WE HANDLE YOUR DATA

1. About This Privacy Policy

Tavique Studio ("Tavique Studio", "we", "our", or "us") is a digital marketing studio based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, focused on helping hotels and resorts improve their digital presence, enquiries and marketing performance.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or contact us through our enquiry forms, WhatsApp, email or other available communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Information included in your enquiry or note
  • Information about your hotel, resort or business when voluntarily provided

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to understand your enquiry and respond to you.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Contact you regarding your request
  • Understand your property's marketing requirements
  • Discuss our services and potential collaboration
  • Provide proposals, information or follow-up communication
  • Improve our website and enquiry process

4. Website Enquiry Forms

When you submit information through our enquiry form, the information may be used to contact you regarding your enquiry and potential marketing services.

We do not sell or intentionally rent your personal information to third parties.

5. WhatsApp, Email and Other Communication

If you contact Tavique Studio through WhatsApp, email, telephone or another communication channel, we may use the information you provide to respond to your request and continue communication regarding your enquiry.

Third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Google or email providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Analytics and Website Technologies

Our website may use analytics, advertising and measurement technologies to understand website usage, campaign performance and visitor behaviour.

These technologies may include Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, advertising platforms and similar measurement tools.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website or marketing activities may use third-party services such as Google, Meta, WhatsApp, hosting providers, analytics platforms or other technology providers.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

9. Data Retention

We may retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, communicate with prospective clients and fulfil legitimate business requirements.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to applicable legal or business requirements.

10. Your Choices

You are not required to provide personal information simply to browse our website.

However, if you choose to submit an enquiry, providing accurate contact information allows us to respond to your request.

You may contact us if you want to ask about personal information we may hold about you or request correction or deletion where applicable.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
